High Interest Papers

Capillary surfaces and free-boundary constructions

  • Capillary L_p-curvature problem by Yingxiang Hu, Mohammad N. Ivaki
  • Capillary minimal slicing and scalar curvature rigidity by Dongyeong Ko, Xuan Yao
  • New minimal surfaces in the sphere from capillary minimal cones by Benjy Firester, Raphael Tsiamis

These works push capillary and free-boundary techniques in directions directly useful for minimal/CMC theory: Hu–Ivaki obtain robust boundary gradient control and existence for the even capillary (L_p)-curvature problem for (1<p<k+1) and contact angles (\theta\in(0,\pi/2)), a key analytic input for capillary PDE. Ko–Yao introduce capillary minimal slicing to derive scalar curvature rigidity on manifolds with boundary, suggesting a capillary analogue of Schoen–Yau minimal slicing that could feed into capillary min–max. Firester–Tsiamis construct new embedded minimal hypersurfaces in spheres by doubling links of capillary/free-boundary cones, bridging capillary limits with free-boundary minimal cones and even the one-phase Bernoulli problem—rich ground for variational and gluing methods.

Mean curvature flow and free-boundary/relativistic settings

  • High Codimension Curve Shortening Flow with Free Boundary by Huy The Nguyen, Artemis A. Vogiatzi
  • Classification of quadratically pinched self-shrinkers in higher codimension by Debora Impera, Michele Rimoldi, Francesco Ruatta
  • High codimension mean curvature flow of spacelike-convex submanifolds with one spacelike codimension by Ben Andrews, Qiyu Zhou

New tools for singularity analysis and classification in curvature flows: Nguyen–Vogiatzi develop boundary entropy and blow-up analysis for free-boundary curve shortening in high codimension, pinning Type I models to the shrinking semicircle and excluding Grim Reaper under entropy (<2). Impera–Rimoldi–Ruatta give a sharp self-shrinker classification under the Andrews–Baker-type pinching with optimal constant (4/(3n)), effectively reducing to codimension one and yielding generalized cylinders—a clean, elliptic route tailored to shrinkers. Andrews–Zhou extend Huisken/Gage–Hamilton-type convergence to spacelike-convex submanifolds in (\mathbb{R}^{n+1,k}), preserving pinching/noncollapsing and proving spherical asymptotics in a maximally spacelike slice; techniques may inform anisotropic or constrained flows.

Min–max PMC hypersurfaces: topology and singular sets

  • Quantitative Estimates on the Topology and Singular Set of Prescribed Mean Curvature Hypersurfaces by Nicolau S. Aiex, Sean McCurdy, Paul Minter

Extends Song’s Morse index–Betti number control from minimal to PMC hypersurfaces and sharpens singular set estimates in high dimensions. For PMC varifolds with area bound and (g\in C^{1,\alpha}), they obtain [ \sum_{i=0}^n b^i(V) \le C\,(1+\mathrm{index}(V)),\quad 3\le n+1\le 7, ] [ M^{*\,n-7}(\mathrm{sing}(V)) \le C\,(1+\mathrm{index}(V)),\quad n+1\ge 8, ] with applicability to recent min–max PMC constructions (Bellettini–Wickramasekera). Directly relevant for quantitative control in variational production of PMC hypersurfaces.

GMT and rectifiability in free-discontinuity problems

  • Uniform rectifiability of brittle fractures in linear elasticity by Camille Labourie

Introduces a “plenty of big projections” route to uniform rectifiability for Griffith cracks, bypassing coarea tools unavailable for the symmetric gradient. This GMT advance on free-discontinuity sets may transfer to singular-set analysis in geometric variational problems (e.g., Mumford–Shah analogues for phase fields/Allen–Cahn approximations).

Stability and spectral bounds for minimal-type submanifolds

  • On the first eigenvalue of the area Jacobi operator for complex curves in Kähler surfaces by Zhenxiao Xie

Obtains a Lichnerowicz-type lower bound for the first Jacobi eigenvalue on complex curves: (\Lambda_1 \ge 2\,\mathfrak{Ric}), specializing to (\Lambda_1 \ge 2\mathfrak{c}) on Kähler–Einstein surfaces, and characterizes equality (attained for genus (g\le 1)). Strengthens stability control for minimal/Willmore-type surfaces in Kähler settings, relevant to index/eigenvalue constraints in variational constructions.

Medium Interest Papers

Variational phase-field/elastic curve couplings

  • Coupling between Phase Separation and Geometry on a Closed Elastic Curve: Free Energy Minimization and Dynamics by Hanchun Wang, Ronojoy Adhikari, Michael E. Cates

Couples Willmore-type bending with Cahn–Hilliard on closed filaments; numerics reveal metastable states driven by curvature–composition coupling. Conceptually adjacent to Allen–Cahn/phase-field approaches to curvature-driven interfaces.

Calibrations and no-gap phenomena

  • Calibrations for the Sasaki volume on odd spheres and the no-gap problem by Jonas Matuzas

Uses calibrations on (UTS^{n}) to minimize the Sasaki volume of unit vector fields, proving no Lavrentiev gap and identifying rigidity conditions. Methodologically interesting for calibrated geometry and integral currents.

Nonlocal geometric energies for normals/tangents

  • Optimal sets for a geometric oscillation energy by Matteo Novaga, Fumihiko Onoue, Emanuele Paolini

Studies a nonlocal (p)-oscillation energy of normals/tangents; establishes sharp geometric inequalities and existence/shape of optimizers under perimeter/volume constraints—links to curvature–variation energies.

Sub-Riemannian variational dynamics

  • Magnetic equations on the Heisenberg group: symmetries, solutions and the inverse problem of the calculus of variations by Gabriela Ovando, Mauro Subils

Explicit integration of magnetic geodesics on (H_3) and variational formulations; broader geometric mechanics with potential techniques transferable to constrained geodesic problems.

Boundary rigidity via Reilly/Steklov methods

  • Integral Inequalities and Rigidity for V-Static-Type Equations on Manifolds with Boundary by Maria Andrade

Derives boundary integral inequalities from generalized Reilly identities and Steklov problems, yielding rigidity (e.g., characterizations of geodesic balls). Adjacent to comparison methods used around capillary/CMC boundary problems.
