High Interest Papers

Minimal hypersurfaces in spheres: constructions and curvature gaps

  • Minimal hypersurfaces in spheres generated by isoparametric foliations by Junqi Lai, Guoxin Wei
  • On Simon's third gap conjecture for minimal surfaces in spheres by Weiran Ding, Jianquan Ge, Fagui Li

These works advance the theory of minimal hypersurfaces in ( \mathbb{S}^{n+1} ). Lai–Wei give a flexible ODE reduction via isoparametric foliations of subspheres to produce closed embedded examples of type ( S^1 \times M ), substantially enlarging the known zoo of minimal hypersurfaces beyond classical hypertori. Ding–Ge–Li sharpen Simons-type integral identities to establish a definitive curvature gap for ( |A|^2 \in \left[\tfrac{5}{3},\tfrac{9}{5}\right] ), with rigidity at the endpoints, tightening classification and pinching phenomena directly relevant to min–max outputs in spheres.

Regularity and singularity structure in GMT for area-minimizing/stable hypersurfaces

  • Area minimising hypersurfaces mod (p) do not admit immersed branch points by Paul Minter, Sidney Stanbury

Minter–Stanbury rule out immersed branch points for area-minimizing hypersurfaces mod (p), and upgrade “almost everywhere immersion” to immersion outside a closed set of Hausdorff dimension at most ( n-3 ) (from an ( \mathcal{H}^{n-1} )-null assumption). The argument hinges on stationarity, stability, and an alternating-orientation structure rather than full minimizing properties, sharpening the singular set paradigm for stable/area-minimizing hypersurfaces and informing blow-up analyses connected to min–max and Allen–Cahn limits.

Harmonic morphisms and minimal submanifolds: new area-minimizing hypercones

  • Harmonic morphisms and minimal submanifolds by Oskar Riedler

Riedler characterizes harmonic morphisms via preservation of minimality in codimension (2) and derives reduction mechanisms for other codimensions, culminating in a new family of degree (4) area-minimizing hypercones in ( \mathbb{R}^m ), ( m \ge 32 ). These cones provide explicit singular models relevant to tangent-cone and stability analyses in geometric measure theory and minimal surface regularity.

Calibrated/Lagrangian geometry, stability, and mean curvature flow

  • Special Lagrangian smoothings, Calabi ansatz and stability conditions by Jacopo Stoppa

Stoppa constructs non-compact special Lagrangian pairs admitting smoothings under small complex-structure deformations on a stability locus determined by a slope inequality for periods, and relates the Calabi-symmetric mean curvature flow limit in the unstable case to a Harder–Narasimhan-type decomposition. The stability–flow correspondence echoes min–max/MCF stability phenomena for minimal hypersurfaces and suggests computational pathways via symmetry-reduced flows.

Constant/parallel mean curvature in inhomogeneous ambient geometries

  • Surfaces with parallel mean curvature in warped product spaces by Fernando Manfio, Verônica Reis, Feliciano Vitório

A geometric classification of parallel mean curvature surfaces in warped products ( \mathbb{R}\times_{\rho}\mathbb{Q}^n_{\varepsilon} ) extends the Alencar–do Carmo–Tribuzy framework. This informs the CMC/PMC landscape in variable-curvature ambients, with potential implications for capillarity-type problems and constrained variational settings.

Medium Interest Papers

Rigidity and topology of hypersurfaces in space forms

  • Topological and rigidity results for four-dimensional hypersurfaces in space forms by Davide Dameno, Aaron J. Tyrrell

Characterizations of isoparametric hypersurfaces via the Weyl tensor and sharp bounds linking ( \|A\| ) to Euler characteristic (in minimal/constant scalar curvature settings) point to refined pinching–rigidity mechanisms that parallel sphere-minimal-surface techniques and could inform higher-dimensional analogues.

Flexibility of conformal immersions via the (h)-principle

  • The (1)-parametric (h)-principle for smooth conformal immersions of surfaces by Alaa Boukholkhal

Establishes that conformal immersions of closed surfaces share path components with all immersions, underscoring flexibility in the conformal category. While not directly minimal, this interfaces with the analytic–topological underpinnings of surface theory relevant to variational constructions.

Computational/variational methods for PDE-constrained shape optimization

  • Global convergence of (W^{1,\infty})-steepest descent for PDE constrained shape optimisation with semilinear elliptic equations in function space by Klaus Deckelnick, Philip J. Herbert, Michael Hinze

Proves global convergence of a function-space steepest descent in the ( W^{1,\infty} ) topology for semilinear elliptic constraints. Techniques are pertinent to robust algorithms for geometric variational problems and could transfer to computational schemes for approximating minimal/CMC interfaces.

Monotone-operator techniques for systems with boundary complementarity

  • A Monotone Operator Approach to Separable Mean-Field Games with Mixed Boundary Conditions by AbdulRahman M. Alharbi, Diogo Gomes

Develops a coercive quotient-space and Browder–Minty framework to handle mixed inflow/Signorini-type boundary conditions in first-order MFGs. The operator-theoretic treatment of boundary complementarity resonates with obstacle/contact conditions appearing in capillarity and constrained geometric flows.
